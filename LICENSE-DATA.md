Under CC BY 4.0, users may share and adapt the covered material for any purpose, including commercially, provided that they:

1. give appropriate credit;
2. provide a link to the licence;
3. indicate whether changes were made; and
4. do not imply endorsement by the dataset authors or participating institutions.

## Material covered

Subject to the final Version 1.0 rights review, this licence is intended to cover author-generated:

- canopy-region binary masks and associated coverage statistics;
- treetop-point coordinates and annotation records;
- individual-crown instance masks, polygon geometry and crown-area tables;
- file manifests, checksums, data dictionaries and dataset metadata.

For the current real-sample package, the covered annotation files include binary-mask files, point CSV/JSON files, instance-mask files, crown-area tables, sample indexes, manifests and checksums. Files that embed source imagery are excluded unless separately cleared.

## Material not covered

The following are **not** licensed under CC BY 4.0 by this file unless an individual asset is explicitly marked otherwise:

- third-party satellite, aerial or basemap imagery;
- source images whose redistribution rights have not been verified;
- preview overlays, videos, animations, composite figures and thumbnails that embed such imagery;
- trademarks, institutional names and logos;
- software and scripts, which are covered separately by [`LICENSE-CODE`](LICENSE-CODE).
